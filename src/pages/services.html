Services

Static websites

A fast, honest website that stays working

For small businesses, freelancers, and community groups who want a professional web presence without a content platform, a monthly plugin bill, or a login they'll forget. Hand-written HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript. Secure by design, quick to load, cheap to host, and yours to keep.

  • I design and build a responsive site with clear structure and real copy
  • I help you publish it to your host and confirm everything works
  • Later changes are billed at a lower rate than the initial build

What you get

  • Works on phones, tablets, and desktops
  • Sensible titles, descriptions, and headings for search
  • Fast load times, no bloat
  • The source files, so you own it outright
  • Hosting and domain stay in your name

Two demo sites

Linux and open source

Plain-language help getting onto Linux, and staying there

For curious beginners, privacy-minded people, students, and small teams who want to understand Linux and find strong open source alternatives without the ideology or the noise. I'm pro-choice, not anti-anything. If proprietary software is the right tool, use it. If a free and open option does the job well, I'll lean there.

What we cover

  • Linux basics, distributions, and desktop options in plain words
  • Moving from Windows or macOS with realistic expectations
  • Open source options for browsers, office, notes, photos, and media
  • A balanced stack: keep what's genuinely best, swap what isn't

And the practical part

  • Hands-on setup so the machine is usable for real work, not demos
  • Simple update, backup, and maintenance habits you'll actually keep
  • Omarchy and Hyprland specifically, if that's where you're headed
