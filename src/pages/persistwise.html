Persistwise

Give your writing a longer life than your platform

Blogs move. Hosts fold. Links rot. Persistwise watches your RSS feed and keeps a durable copy of every post, so the words outlast wherever you first put them.

persistwise.com

What it does, plainly

Keep publishing

Nothing changes about where or how you write. Persistwise only needs the feed.

Keep a copy

Each new post is archived as local HTML, snapshotted to the Wayback Machine, and pinned to IPFS. Three places, three failure modes, all different.

Keep it simple

One small service with a yearly price, not a publishing system to migrate into.

How it's built

Stack
Rails 8, Hotwire, SQLite, Solid Queue. Small on purpose.
Hosting
Railway.
Preservation
Local HTML and WARC, Wayback Machine Save Page Now, IPFS pinning. Arweave is on the list.
Price
$40 a year for an archive. About $70 if you want typeset PDFs of each post too.
