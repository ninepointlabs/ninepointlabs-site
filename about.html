About

The story behind Nine Point Labs

Nine Point Labs is Tim Apple's build-in-public workshop for experiments, ideas, and practical projects. It is where technology, family life, service, and faith all meet in one honest place.

Meet Tim

Tim is a combat veteran and semi-retired technologist who works part-time at an MSP. The other half of life is full-on dad mode: playing Mr. Mom and raising the kiddos alongside his wife.

When he is not geeking out with his eyes glued to a monitor, Tim is in Tae Kwon Do with his daughters. Starting late in life has been equal parts joy and tendon pain, but worth every minute.

Why Nine Point?

Tim is a member of the Baha'i Faith, and the number nine carries special meaning in Baha'i thought. Nine is the highest single-digit number and is often used as a symbol of completeness and unity, especially the unity of humanity.

That spirit of unity is the heartbeat of Nine Point Labs: bring useful ideas together, build with optimism, and leave people better off than we found them.

Positive influences

Tim's favorite show is Ted Lasso, and his favorite artist is Andy Grammer. Both are steady reminders that optimism can be practical, kindness can be strong, and grit does not have to come with cynicism.
