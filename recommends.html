Recommends

Things I pay for and would tell a friend about

No affiliate links, no sponsorships, no ranking. These are the tools I use most days and would recommend if you asked me over coffee. The list changes when my habits do.

The current list

O

Omarchy

The Linux setup I live in. Arch, Hyprland, a Quickshell bar you can extend, and opinionated defaults that mostly match mine. Half the work on this site is plugins for it.

omarchy.org

H

HEY

Email and calendar. It's where my journal lives too, which is why there's a HEY calendar widget on the Work page. Opinionated, and I happen to agree with most of the opinions.

hey.com

K

Kagi

Paid search with no ads and no tracking. Results are good enough that I stopped noticing I'd switched, which is the highest compliment I can give a search engine.

kagi.com

E

Ente

Open source, end-to-end encrypted photo backup. Family photos treated like memories rather than ad inventory.

ente.io

P

Proton

Privacy-first mail, calendar, VPN, and drive. A good default for someone who wants modern tools without the data-harvesting business model behind them.

proton.me

F

Fastmail

Reliable, privacy-respecting email with a long track record and a great API. I liked it enough to build a terminal client for it.

fastmail.com

B

Firefox and Brave

Firefox for an independent, open web. Brave when you want tracker and ad blocking out of the box without configuring anything. Either beats the default.

firefox, brave.com

N

Nostr

Not a product, a protocol. Your identity is a key you hold, and no company owns the network. Start with a client like Primal or Damus, and find me at the address in the footer.

nostr.com
