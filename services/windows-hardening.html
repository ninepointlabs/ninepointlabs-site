Service

Windows Hardening & Privacy Protection

We help make your Windows PC more secure and more private without breaking the tools you rely on. We reduce unnecessary telemetry, tighten security settings, and configure the system so your data is treated with respect.

What it is

This is a practical hardening service for real users. We focus on useful improvements that strengthen privacy and security while keeping your computer familiar and usable.

What we do

  • Disable or minimize Windows telemetry and background data collection
  • Configure privacy settings at both operating system and application levels
  • Remove or disable unnecessary pre-installed software and bloatware
  • Set up local accounts instead of mandatory cloud accounts where possible
  • Recommend and configure privacy-respecting alternatives for common software
  • Improve security settings including firewall rules, update policies, and user account controls

What we do not do

We do not force one-size-fits-all changes or break your workflow. Every adjustment is tailored to how you actually use your machine, and we explain each change in plain language before and after implementation.

Who this is for

Anyone who uses Windows and wants more control over system behavior and personal data, from privacy-conscious individuals to small business owners managing daily operations.
