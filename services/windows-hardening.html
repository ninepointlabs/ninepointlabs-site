Service

Privacy Setup

We help people set up privacy-focused tools, reduce invasive defaults, and build safer habits without forcing them to replace the systems they already know. We work with Windows, macOS, and Linux, and harden computers where it makes sense.

What it is

This is a practical consultation service for real users who want more privacy and security without being pushed into an all-or-nothing setup. We focus on useful changes that fit your actual devices, comfort level, and daily workflow.

What we do

  • Help you choose and set up privacy-focused tools for email, browsing, search, storage, and communication
  • Turn off or reduce telemetry, tracking, and other invasive defaults where possible
  • Harden Windows, macOS, or Linux systems based on what you already use and need
  • Review account, browser, and device settings to improve privacy without breaking your workflow
  • Teach practical security habits so you can make better day-to-day decisions on your own
  • Work within your current hardware and software instead of forcing unnecessary replacement or migration

How we work

We do not force one-size-fits-all changes or break your workflow. Every recommendation is tailored to how you actually use your machine, and we explain the tradeoffs in plain language before and after implementation.

Who this is for

Anyone who wants more control over their devices and personal data, from privacy-conscious individuals to small business owners who need practical guidance they can understand and keep using.
