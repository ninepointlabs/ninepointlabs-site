Service

Static Websites

One of our favorite ideas in the Nine Point Labs workshop is simple: build websites that are fast, dependable, and easy to own. No plugin jungle. No fragile stack. Just clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that does the job and keeps doing it.

What it is

This service is for teams and solo builders who want a professional web presence without signing up for avoidable complexity. Static sites are secure by design, quick to load, and refreshingly straightforward to maintain.

How it works

  1. Initial consultation: We discuss your goals, audience, and core story.
  2. Design and build: We create a responsive site with clear structure and purposeful copy.
  3. Launch: We help publish the site to your chosen host and confirm everything works.
  4. Incremental changes: When you need updates, we handle them quickly at a lower rate.

Pricing model

Initial build

Your website is scoped and billed as a one-time project. You pay for craftsmanship, not a recurring platform tax.

Incremental changes

Edits and additions are billed at a lower rate than initial build work. Hosting and domain costs stay in your name, and we guide when needed.

What you get

  • Responsive design that works on phones, tablets, and desktop devices
  • SEO basics such as page titles, descriptions, and sensible heading structure
  • Fast load times with lightweight code and minimal overhead
  • Clean hand-written source files (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript)
  • Handover of the source files so you keep full ownership
