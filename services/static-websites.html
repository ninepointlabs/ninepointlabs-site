Service

Static Websites

We build clean, fast, low-maintenance static websites that are easy to own and easy to trust. No bloated CMS, no plugin sprawl, and no unnecessary complexity. Just well-structured HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that loads quickly and stays reliable.

What it is

This service is for clients who want a professional web presence without signing up for a fragile stack of moving parts. Static sites are secure by design, perform well on nearly any host, and are straightforward to maintain over time.

How it works

  1. Initial consultation: We discuss your goals, audience, and core content.
  2. Design and build: We create a responsive site with clear structure and polished copy.
  3. Launch: We help publish the site to your chosen host and confirm everything works.
  4. Incremental changes: When you need updates, we handle them quickly at a lower rate.

Pricing model

Initial build

Your website is billed as a one-time project price based on scope. You pay for the build, not a recurring subscription.

Incremental changes

Edits, updates, and small additions are billed at a lower price than the initial build. Hosting and domain costs are paid directly by you, and we can advise if needed.

What you get

  • Responsive design that works on phones, tablets, and desktop devices
  • SEO basics such as page titles, descriptions, and sensible heading structure
  • Fast load times with lightweight code and minimal overhead
  • Clean hand-written source files (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript)
  • Handover of the source files so you keep full ownership
