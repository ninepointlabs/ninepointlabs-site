What MSPs Do

Managed Service Providers (MSPs) are outside teams that proactively keep a business's technology healthy, secure, and predictable.

Plain-language definition

An MSP monitors and manages IT systems for another organization, usually for a recurring monthly fee. Instead of waiting for break-fix emergencies, MSPs focus on prevention, continuity, and fast response.

Common MSP responsibilities

  • Security updates, patching, endpoint protection, and threat response
  • Help desk support for daily user issues and account access problems
  • Backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity planning
  • Network and cloud management with uptime and performance monitoring

Why businesses choose MSPs

  • Predictable IT costs with fewer surprise outages
  • Access to specialized skills without hiring a large internal team
  • Faster troubleshooting and better day-to-day reliability
  • More time for the company to focus on its own mission
