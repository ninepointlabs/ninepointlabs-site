Partners and Recommendations

Services we use and trust

These are tools and services we personally use and recommend. We are not obligated to list them, and we do not recommend them out of hype. We recommend them because they are reliable, privacy-respecting, and built around user interests.

Recommended services

Proton (Proton.me)

Privacy-first email, calendar, VPN, and cloud storage for people who want to move away from data-harvesting ecosystems.

Visit Proton

Ente (Ente.io)

Open-source, end-to-end encrypted photo storage and backup. A practical privacy-respecting alternative to ad-driven photo platforms.

Visit Ente

Kagi (Kagi.com)

A search engine focused on quality results, no ads, and no tracking. Ideal for users who want a cleaner and faster search experience.

Visit Kagi

Fastmail

Reliable, privacy-respecting email with a long track record. Strong choice for custom domains and day-to-day personal mail.

Visit Fastmail

Firefox

The independent open-source browser from Mozilla. A solid default browser for users who want a more private and less commercial web experience.

Visit Firefox

Brave

A privacy-focused browser with built-in tracker and ad blocking. Good option for users who want stronger default protection right away.

Visit Brave
