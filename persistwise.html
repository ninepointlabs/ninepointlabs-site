Persistwise

Writing that outlasts the platform

Persistwise quietly watches the RSS feeds where people publish and archives each new post as it appears. It is for writers who want to keep using Hey, Substack, their own site, or a social feed without leaving the long-term record entirely to someone else.

Visit Persistwise

A durable record for every post

Each archived post keeps its original URL, current home, and preservation date in one simple record. Persistwise saves a local copy, requests a public snapshot through the Wayback Machine, and keeps a pinned copy on IPFS.

Quiet infrastructure, carefully made

Persistwise is built to do one job reliably: preserve writing without asking authors to change where or how they publish. The service polls feeds, records new posts, and keeps the archive understandable years later.
