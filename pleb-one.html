Pleb One

The Nostr chapter is still open

Pleb One started as a Nostr initiative, and we still support it. Nine Point Labs pivoted into a broader experiment studio, but Nostr remains part of the story and part of the work. We still run infrastructure, still ship experiments, and still believe in open protocols that give people more control.

What is Nostr?

Nostr is an open protocol for social communication. Instead of one company owning the network, people use different clients and publish through relays. Your identity is tied to cryptographic keys, and the ecosystem stays portable because no single platform owns your account graph.

  • Open protocol, not a single app or company
  • Many clients can read and write to the same network
  • Relays carry events across the ecosystem
  • Users control identity through key pairs

About the pivot

We expanded from a Nostr-first initiative into Nine Point Labs so we could incubate more ideas across web, systems, and open source work. That pivot did not mean abandoning Nostr. It meant giving ourselves a bigger workshop while continuing to support the projects and people who helped start this journey.
