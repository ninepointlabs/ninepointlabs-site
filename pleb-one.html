Pleb One

Where this started

Pleb One was a Nostr-first project: a relay, some experiments, and a lot of learning in public. Nine Point Labs grew out of it so I could build more widely. The relay never went anywhere.

If you're new to Nostr

It's an open protocol for social communication. No company owns the network. You use whichever client you like, publish through relays, and your identity is a key pair you hold. That last part is why I care about it, and why my Nostr tools all keep the key on your own machine.
